LicenseRef-Qt-Commercial
========================

This is NOT a licence text. It is the record of what this identifier refers to, which is the
correct thing to put here when the referenced text is not ours to distribute.

Files in this repository that carry

    SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Qt-Commercial OR GPL-3.0-only

come from Qt's own source tree, where The Qt Company offers them under a disjunction: either a
commercial licence agreement with The Qt Company, or the GNU General Public License version 3.

The commercial agreement is a private contract between The Qt Company and its licensees. Its text
is not published, this project holds no such agreement, and nothing here grants any right under it.

**This project relies on the GPL-3.0-only branch of that disjunction**, and the full text of that
branch is distributed with this repository, in `LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt`, as GPLv3 §4 requires of
anyone conveying the source. A recipient who holds a commercial Qt agreement may of course rely on
that branch instead; that is between them and The Qt Company.

Where this material lives, and why it is here at all, is recorded in `THIRD-PARTY.md`. It is test
material: it is never linked into a product artifact, and `license-package` refuses a package that
contains it.
